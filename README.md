# TactiQ

### AI-Powered Football Intelligence Platform

TactiQ is a football intelligence platform combining football
analytics, computer vision, machine learning, generative AI,
and mobile technology.

## Vision

Help football analysts, coaches, scouts, and enthusiasts
understand professional football through data-driven insights,
tactical analysis, and AI-powered intelligence.

## Core Modules

- Club Intelligence
- Competition Intelligence
- Match Intelligence
- Player Intelligence
- Tactical Intelligence
- Video Intelligence
- AI Tactical Analyst
- Recruitment Intelligence
